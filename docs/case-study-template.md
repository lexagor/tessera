# Design-Partner Case Study Template

Use only with written customer consent. Do not invent a customer, quote, metric, or reviewer
acceptance. Until a signed case study exists, say “design-partner recruitment” and “no public
customer outcome yet.”

## Required inputs

- Customer name/logo usage approval and quote approval.
- The one action instrumented and the business process it affected.
- Before/after workflow, integration time, and number of actions.
- Security reviewer feedback and whether the evidence pack/verification page was accepted.
- Measurable outcome (review cycle time, deal progression, incident reduction) with source.
- Redactions for tenant names, secrets, amounts, and personal data.
- Documented limitations and what Tessera did not prove.

## Draft outline

1. **Context:** customer, agent, enterprise buyer, security-review blocker.
2. **Action:** exact tool and authority boundary instrumented.
3. **Implementation:** integration path, time, and sandbox-only credentials.
4. **Evidence:** what the reviewer checked and accepted, with a redacted artifact.
5. **Outcome:** measured change, quote, and remaining gaps.
6. **Guardrails:** what the case study does not claim (no SOC 2, no tamper-proof storage, no
   universal provider coverage).

Store the signed consent and fact-check sheet with the case study. If any input is missing, do
not publish the case study.
