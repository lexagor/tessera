What this proves

The broker signed this exact call, the principal, the policy version/hash, the evaluated inputs, the tool and arguments hash, the denial decision, the prior record, and the timestamp. Changing any signed field invalidates the signature.

What this does not prove

This is a policy-denial record, not an executed refund. No credential was issued, no provider call was made, and no money moved. A valid signature does not prove the underlying business intent was lawful, and Tessera records are tamper-evident, not tamper-proof.
