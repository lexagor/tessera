# Fundraise and Legal Checklist

**Status:** founder-controlled inputs. Do not publish an ask, SAFE terms, team slide, or IP
ownership statement until the founder confirms them in writing.

## Company and IP

- [ ] Confirm legal entity, jurisdiction, incorporation documents, and good standing.
- [ ] Founder stock/vesting, IP assignment, contractor/employee PIIA, and conflict checks.
- [ ] Open-source license review and trademark/name clearance.
- [ ] Confirm ownership of domains, GitHub org, cloud accounts, and Auth0 tenant.
- [ ] Data-processing roles, privacy policy, terms, DPA, and subprocessor list reviewed by counsel.
- [ ] Security disclosure policy and incident contact are monitored.

## Round terms

- [ ] Final raise amount and minimum close; no placeholder in the deck.
- [ ] Instrument (SAFE/equity), valuation cap/discount, pro rata, MFN, and conversion terms.
- [ ] Use of funds tied to design partners, security review, and infrastructure.
- [ ] Cap table, option pool, and dilution modeled.
- [ ] Investor updates and data-room access policy.

## Team slide

- [ ] Founder names, roles, locations, and time commitment.
- [ ] Relevant security/engineering proof, not inflated titles.
- [ ] Hiring plan and key-person risk.
- [ ] Advisors/contractors with signed agreements.

## Data room

- [ ] Product demo and live verification path.
- [ ] Architecture, threat model, controls matrix, tests, and evidence-pack sample.
- [ ] Financial model with sourced assumptions.
- [ ] Legal entity, IP, privacy/DPA, and subprocessor documents.
- [ ] Customer/design-partner pipeline with consent and no invented logos.

## Do not claim

- SOC 2 certification, a completed external pen test, paying customers, named partners, or
  reviewer acceptance before the artifacts exist.
- A final valuation/raise amount that the founder has not confirmed.
- Production HA, KMS/HSM custody, or a contractual SLA before the operations are live.
