What this proves

Changing one signed field is detectable without trusting a server or asking Tessera to re-sign anything. The verifier checks the EIP-712 signature and the authorization/outcome binding locally.

What this does not prove

Tessera records are tamper-evident, not tamper-proof. This page demonstrates detection of a modified copy; it does not make the original record immutable, and it does not prove the business event was lawful.
